    About Us

    Rooted in 1986. Growing every year since.

    Our Story

    Good Shepherd Matriculation Higher Secondary School opened its doors in Korattur in 1986 with a simple aim: guide every child attentively, patiently, and toward good ground.

    Our Vision

    To nurture confident, compassionate learners who rise to meet the world with knowledge, integrity and the courage to lead.

    Our Mission

    • Deliver academic excellence across LKG through Class XII.
    • Build character through discipline, service and shared responsibility.
    • Provide a safe, encouraging campus where every student is known by name.
    • Prepare students for board examinations and for life beyond them.

    Motto

    "Arise and Shine"

    A daily reminder to our students that every morning is a fresh chance to grow — academically, morally and personally.

    Academics

    A curriculum built stage by stage

    Primary

    LKG – Class V

    Play-based learning that builds phonics, numeracy and curiosity, with English, Tamil, Mathematics, Environmental Science and creative arts.

    Middle School

    Class VI – VIII

    A widened subject base — Science, Social Science, Mathematics, Computer Science and two languages — with lab work and project-based assessment.

    Secondary

    Class IX – X

    Board-aligned Matriculation syllabus in Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Mathematics, Social Science, English and Tamil, with structured exam preparation.

    Higher Secondary

    Class XI – XII

    Streams in Biology, Computer Science and Commerce, taught by subject specialists with regular model examinations.

    How We Assess

    Students are assessed through periodic tests, unit exams and term examinations. Rank cards are uploaded directly by administration and accessible via Student Login.
